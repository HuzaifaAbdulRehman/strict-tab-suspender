How suspended tabs restore
Permission required for click to restore

Chrome describes the required tabs permission as “Read your browsing history.”

It lets this extension read the original address and title only when suspending that tab. Both are percent-encoded in the local suspended page address so they survive a browser restart. It is not encrypted and can appear in Chrome's address bar, session restore, and history. The original address and title are not saved in extension storage or sent over the network.

Privacy and limitations

Settings and only the latest aggregate sweep summary stay on this device. No URLs, titles, tab IDs, page content, or browsing history are saved in extension storage.

Native-mode tabs reload when opened. Click-mode tabs wait for an explicit Restore tab action.

This extension cannot detect unsaved forms or in-memory work. Save important work first.

Pinned and audible tabs are always protected and cannot be disabled.
